My promise

Free. Simple. Respectful. The App is 100% free. It contains no advertisements, subscriptions, paywalls, paid features, user accounts, analytics SDKs, advertising identifiers, profiling, or user tracking. Optional support never unlocks features or changes access.

Files you choose

The App reads only files you deliberately select through Android's file picker. Depending on what you choose, those files may contain personal or sensitive information. Viewing, PDF editing, and conversion happen locally on your device. The App does not upload file names or contents to the Developer or to a Frogdadday server.

Supported previews may include PDF, Office documents, text and markup shown as non-executed text, images, audio, and video. Audio and video players receive local file paths only. Unsupported files or codecs produce an error instead of being sent elsewhere.

Saving and temporary copies

When you save or convert a file, Android's file picker lets you choose the destination. The App creates a copy and does not replace the original file in place.

Some picked media requires a bounded temporary copy in the App's cache so Android can play it. App-owned preview copies are removed after the viewer closes. Your chosen saved outputs remain where you put them until you delete them.

Unsaved PDF recovery

To protect unsaved PDF edits if Android interrupts the App, Frog File Viewer may keep one recovery copy in private app storage. It stays on the device, is replaced as editing continues, expires automatically after 24 hours, and is removed after you save or discard the work.

Internet and Android permissions

The distributed release App does not have Android's Internet permission or network-state permission and cannot make in-app internet requests. It does not request broad access to your storage. Android's system file picker grants access only to files you choose.

The bundled local media player may keep the processor awake during playback so audio or video is not interrupted when the screen changes state. This does not provide access to personal information or a network.

Development-only debug builds may include Flutter's Internet permission for hot reload and debugging. Those developer builds are not the Google Play release described by this policy.

Advertising, analytics, and tracking

The App contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, profiling, social-media, or third-party tracking SDKs. It does not use cookies, fingerprinting, or create a user profile.

Retention, deletion, and device backups

The Developer has no remote copy of your files or activity and cannot view, recover, or remotely delete information on your phone. You can delete exported files using your chosen file app. Clearing the App's storage or uninstalling removes App-private data under Android's normal behavior.

Android, your device manufacturer, or a storage provider you select may include files or App data in backups or device transfers according to your settings and that provider's privacy terms. Frogdadday does not control those services.

Privacy page, support, and outside services

The Privacy policy and optional Buy Me a Coffee buttons ask Android to open a webpage in your external browser only after you press them. The App itself does not load either website.

This policy page is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection and usage information under its privacy statement. This page contains no added analytics, advertisements, cookies, forms, external assets, or tracking scripts.

If you choose Buy Me a Coffee, that service and your browser may process connection, payment, and supporter information under their own policies. Support is never required and never unlocks features. The App does not receive payment information.

Google Play, Android, your device manufacturer, file providers, and other apps you open at your request may independently process information needed to provide their services under their own terms and privacy policies.

Security

The App uses Android's app isolation, system file picker, private storage, and bounded file-processing limits. No storage method can guarantee absolute security. Keep your device protected and install operating-system security updates when available.

Children's privacy

The App does not transmit personal information to the Developer from any user, including children. It has no account, chat, public profile, or in-app sharing service.

Direct communications

If you contact the Developer outside the App, the Developer receives only the information you choose to provide. It is used to respond, is not sold, and is kept only as long as reasonably necessary for the inquiry or applicable legal obligations. Do not send private documents unless they are necessary for your question.
